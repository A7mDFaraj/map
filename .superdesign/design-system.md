# SCY Design System

## Product Context

- Product: digital map for youth associations in Saudi Arabia.
- Owner: Specialized Council for Youth NGOs.
- Primary route in this task: `/new2`.
- Experience direction: official, premium, clear, data-oriented, Arabic-first, RTL.

## Brand Assets

- Full colored logo: `/brand/logo/logo-colored.svg`
- Full white logo: `/brand/logo/logo-white.svg`
- Minimal symbol: `/brand/logo/logo-minimal.svg`
- Frame pattern: `/brand/logo/frame.svg`
- Pattern asset: `/brand/logo/pattern.svg`

## Tokens

- Primary: `#233A77`
- Secondary: `#1C81AC`
- Accent: `#3EB985`
- Ink: `#12172e`
- Muted ink: `#5b6178`
- Surface: `#ffffff`
- Alternate surface: `#f6f7fb`
- Border: `#e4e6f0`
- Font: Tajawal via `next/font/google`

## Visual Rules

- Keep the SCY palette dominant and do not introduce unrelated colors.
- Use the brand pattern subtly; it should support content, not compete with it.
- Use the real SCY logo assets, never generic placeholders for the brand.
- Prefer rounded line icons and clean institutional surfaces.
- Keep RTL spacing and alignment intentional.
- Motion should be restrained: reveal, translate, and soft hover states only.
